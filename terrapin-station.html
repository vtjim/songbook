Terrapin Station

Grateful Dead · 1977 Studio Album

Quick Facts

  • Released July 27, 1977 on Arista — the band's first album for the label
  • Produced by Keith Olsen; recorded at Sound City (Van Nuys), Automated Sound (NYC), and London studios including AIR, Trident and Abbey Road
  • Peaked at #28 on the Billboard 200; certified Gold in September 1987
  • Olsen added orchestral and choral arrangements without the band's consent — Garcia said he'd 'put the Grateful Dead in a dress'
  • Side two is the single 16:23 'Terrapin Part 1': Lady with a Fan / Terrapin Station / Terrapin / Terrapin Transit / At a Siding / Terrapin Flyer / Refrain
