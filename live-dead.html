Live/Dead

Grateful Dead · 1969 First Live Album

Quick Facts

  • Released November 10, 1969 on Warner Bros.–Seven Arts — the band's first live album and fourth overall
  • Recorded January 26 – March 2, 1969 at the Avalon Ballroom and Fillmore West; produced by the band with Bob Matthews and Betty Cantor
  • The first live rock album recorded on 16-track — their soundman had a microphone splitter built to feed the PA and the recorder simultaneously with no loss of quality
  • Peaked at #62 on the Billboard 200
  • The four vinyl sides were sequenced for automatic record changers so the album plays back as one continuous concert; the 23-minute 'Dark Star' fills side one
