Reckoning

Grateful Dead · 1981 Live Acoustic Album

Quick Facts

  • 1981 live double album — the band's 6th live release (17th overall)
  • Fully acoustic; recorded live Sept–Oct 1980 at the Warfield (San Francisco) and Radio City Music Hall (NYC)
  • Companion release to Dead Set (1981), the electric album from the same shows
  • 16 tracks, ~95 minutes; "Oh Babe, It Ain't No Lie" was cut from early CD pressings for space, later restored
  • Charted #43 on the Billboard Pop Albums chart; "Dire Wolf" reached #37 on Mainstream Rock
