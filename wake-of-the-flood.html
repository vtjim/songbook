Wake of the Flood

Grateful Dead · 1973 Studio Album

Quick Facts

  • Released October 15, 1973 — the band's first album on their own independent label, Grateful Dead Records, after leaving Warner Bros.
  • Recorded August 4–15, 1973 at The Record Plant in Sausalito on 24-track equipment; peaked at #18 on the Billboard 200
  • The first Dead studio album without Pigpen, who died in March 1973; Keith Godchaux on keys, Donna Jean Godchaux on vocals
  • Seven tracks, closing with the multi-part 'Weather Report Suite' (Prelude / Part I / Part II 'Let It Grow')
  • 'Let Me Sing Your Blues Away' is Keith Godchaux's only songwriting credit on a Dead studio album — played live just six times, in September 1973
