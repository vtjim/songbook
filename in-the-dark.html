In the Dark

Grateful Dead · 1987 Studio Album

Quick Facts

  • Released July 6, 1987 on Arista, produced by Jerry Garcia and John Cutler
  • Peaked at #6 on the Billboard 200 — the band's only top-ten album — and went double platinum
  • Basic tracks were cut January 6–13, 1987 with the band playing on stage at the Marin Veterans' Memorial Auditorium, in a darkened empty theater with no audience, for a live feel at studio fidelity
  • Garcia on the venue: "There's something about the formal atmosphere in there that makes us work."
  • 'Touch of Grey' hit #9 on the Hot 100 — their only top-forty single — went #1 on Mainstream Rock, and its video became an MTV staple
  • 'Tons of Steel' is Brent Mydland's sole writing credit here; 'Hell in a Bucket' is Weir/Mydland/Barlow
