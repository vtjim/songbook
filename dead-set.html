Dead Set

Grateful Dead · 1981 Live Electric Album

Quick Facts

  • Released August 26, 1981 on Arista — the electric companion to Reckoning, drawn from the same Sept–Oct 1980 shows
  • Recorded at the Warfield Theatre (San Francisco) and Radio City Music Hall (NYC)
  • 15 tracks including the Drums → Space percussion-and-improv segment
  • Reached #29 on the Billboard Pop Albums chart (1981)
  • Original CD pressings dropped "Space" to fit a single disc; later reissues restored it
