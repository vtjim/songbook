Anthem of the Sun

Grateful Dead · 1968 Studio Album

Quick Facts

  • Released July 18, 1968 on Warner Bros.–Seven Arts — the band's second album
  • Built as a studio/live collage: Garcia, Lesh, and soundman Dan Healy interlaced 1967–68 concert tapes with studio takes, sometimes layered simultaneously
  • Peaked at #87 on the Billboard Top LPs chart
  • A Phil Lesh–supervised remix issued in the early 1970s has circulated alongside the original 1968 mix ever since
  • Ranked #288 on Rolling Stone's 500 Greatest Albums of All Time (2003 and 2012 lists)
