Blues for Allah

Grateful Dead · 1975 Studio Album

Quick Facts

  • The band's eighth studio album, released September 1, 1975 on Grateful Dead Records
  • Reached #12 on the Billboard Pop Albums chart with a 13-week run — their highest-charting album to that point
  • Recorded at Bob Weir's home studio in Mill Valley rather than a commercial studio
  • Mickey Hart, who left the band in 1971, was invited back for these sessions and officially rejoined shortly after release
  • Several tracks are instrumentals: Slipknot!, King Solomon's Marbles, Stronger than Dirt or Milkin' the Turkey, Sage & Spirit, and Sand Castles & Glass Camels
