Europe '72

Grateful Dead · 1972 Live Triple Album

Quick Facts

  • A triple live album released November 5, 1972 on Warner Bros., drawn from the band's 22-date European tour (April 7 – May 26, 1972)
  • Peaked at #24 on the Billboard 200 and went Double Platinum — one of the first triple rock albums certified gold
  • The last Grateful Dead album with Pigpen, who died in March 1973, and the first with Keith and Donna Jean Godchaux
  • Despite being live, the tracks carry substantial studio overdubs, especially vocals; several Garcia-sung songs were pitched sharp by as much as a half-step
  • The whole tour was later issued as Europe '72: The Complete Recordings, a 73-CD box of all 22 shows that sold out as a pre-order in under four days
