Go to Heaven

Grateful Dead · 1980 Studio Album

Quick Facts

  • The band's eleventh studio album, released April 28, 1980 on Arista, produced by Gary Lyons
  • Peaked at #23 on the Billboard Pop Albums chart
  • The first Dead album with Brent Mydland, who sings and co-writes two songs ('Far from Me' and 'Easy to Love You')
  • Their last studio album for seven years, until In the Dark (1987)
  • Nine tracks in about 38 minutes, including 'Antwerp's Placebo', a 38-second Hart/Kreutzmann percussion interlude
