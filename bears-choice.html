Bear's Choice

Grateful Dead · 1973 Live Album — History of the Grateful Dead, Vol. One

Quick Facts

  • Full title: History of the Grateful Dead, Volume One (Bear's Choice); released July 13, 1973 on Warner Bros.
  • Compiled by soundman Owsley 'Bear' Stanley from his own recordings — hence the name
  • Recorded February 13–14, 1970 at the Fillmore East; side one is the acoustic set, side two the electric set
  • Effectively a tribute to Pigpen, who died during production and sings lead on three of the electric tracks; it peaked at #60
  • The Dancing Bears graphic made its first appearance on this album's back cover — and despite the 'Volume One' billing, no further volumes were released
