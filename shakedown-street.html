Shakedown Street

Grateful Dead · 1978 Studio Album

Quick Facts

  • Released November 8, 1978 on Arista, produced by Lowell George (with John Kahn uncredited)
  • Peaked at #41 on the Billboard Pop Albums chart — their first studio album since Aoxomoxoa to miss the Top 40 — though certified Gold in 1987
  • The final Dead studio album with Keith and Donna Jean Godchaux, who left the band a few months after release
  • Mickey Hart took three co-composition credits and pushed the record's disco/polyrhythmic direction
  • Ten tracks, including the Hart/Kreutzmann percussion instrumental 'Serengetti'
