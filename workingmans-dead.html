Workingman's Dead

Grateful Dead · 1970 Studio Album

Quick Facts

  • The band's fourth studio album — 8 tracks, released June 14, 1970 on Warner Bros.
  • Recorded in February 1970 at Pacific High Recording, San Francisco, produced by Bob Matthews, Betty Cantor, and the band
  • Peaked at #27 on the Billboard 200; RIAA-certified Platinum
  • The stylistic pivot to acoustic country/folk with CSN-influenced harmonies; Rolling Stone ranked it #262 on the 2003 '500 Greatest Albums' list
  • 'New Speedway Boogie' answers the December 1969 Altamont disaster; 'Cumberland Blues' has Garcia's only banjo part on a Dead studio song
