Built to Last

Grateful Dead · 1989 Final Studio Album

Quick Facts

  • The band's thirteenth and final studio album, released October 31, 1989 on Arista
  • Recorded February–October 1989 at Club Front and Skywalker Ranch (San Rafael) and Studio X (Petaluma); produced by John Cutler and Jerry Garcia
  • Peaked at #27 on the Billboard 200 and was certified Gold in January 1990; the single 'Foolish Heart' reached #8 on Mainstream Rock
  • Features more Brent Mydland songs than any other Dead album — four, all co-written with John Perry Barlow — and was the last released in his lifetime; he died a year later
  • The LP dropped 'We Can Run' entirely; it is a CD-only track
