American Beauty

Grateful Dead · 1970 Studio Album

Quick Facts

  • Released November 1970 on Warner Bros. — the band's second studio album of that year, after Workingman's Dead
  • Recorded August–September 1970 at Wally Heider Studios, San Francisco, produced by the band with Stephen Barncard
  • Peaked at #30 on the Billboard 200; certified 2× Platinum. The 'Truckin'' single reached #64
  • David Grisman plays mandolin on several tracks — he'd met Garcia at a baseball game
  • 'Truckin'' was recognized by the Library of Congress in 1997 as a national treasure
