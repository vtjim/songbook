From the Mars Hotel

Grateful Dead · 1974 Studio Album

Quick Facts

  • The band's seventh studio album, released June 27, 1974 on their own Grateful Dead Records label
  • Peaked at #16 on the Billboard Pop Albums chart — mainly recorded April 1974 at CBS Studios, San Francisco, chosen for the room's more 'live' sound
  • Cover art by Kelley/Mouse (Stanley Mouse and Alton Kelley), who also designed American Beauty and Europe '72
  • The final studio album before the band's indefinite touring hiatus, which began in October 1974
  • Lesh/Petersen wrote 'Unbroken Chain' and 'Pride of Cucamonga'; Weir/Barlow wrote 'Money Money'; the rest are Garcia/Hunter
