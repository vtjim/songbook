Aoxomoxoa

Grateful Dead · 1969 Studio Album

Quick Facts

  • Released June 20, 1969 on Warner Bros.–Seven Arts — the band's third studio album; #73 on the Billboard 200
  • Recorded Sept 1968 – March 1969 at Pacific Recording (San Mateo) and Pacific High (SF) — one of the first albums made on then-new 16-track technology
  • Garcia and Lesh remixed it in 1971, stripping experimental elements; the remix was the standard version for decades
  • The title is a meaningless palindrome devised by cover artist Rick Griffin and Robert Hunter
  • Eight months of 16-track experimentation ran the cost to about $180,000 — the band's most expensive studio project to that point
