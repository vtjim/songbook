Skull & Roses

Grateful Dead · 1971 Live Double Album

Quick Facts

  • Released September 24, 1971 on Warner Bros. — the band's second live double album, issued with no visible title on the sleeve
  • Known as 'Skull and Roses' from its cover art (after an Edmund Joseph Sullivan illustration); the band wanted it called 'Skull Fuck', which Warner Bros. rejected
  • Peaked at #25 on the Billboard 200 and became the Dead's first gold album
  • Recorded March 24 – April 29, 1971 at Winterland, the Hammerstein Ballroom, and the Fillmore East
  • Carried the famous insert — 'Dead Freaks Unite: Who are you? Where are you? How are you?' — which started the Dead Heads mailing list
  • Features vocal overdubs and organ from Merl Saunders
