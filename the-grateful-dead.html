The Grateful Dead

Grateful Dead · 1967 Debut Studio Album

Quick Facts

  • Released March 17, 1967 on Warner Bros. — the band's debut studio album
  • Recorded in about four days in January 1967 at RCA's Studio A in Hollywood, produced by David Hassinger — San Francisco studios lacked modern equipment
  • Peaked at #73 on the Billboard 200
  • Group compositions were credited to the pseudonym 'McGannahan Skjellyfetti' — a Kenneth Patchen character crossed with the name of Bill Kreutzmann's cat
  • Only two originals — 'The Golden Road' (the single) and Garcia's 'Cream Puff War' (its B-side); the other seven are folk/blues covers
